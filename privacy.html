Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Short version: we don't collect anything. sportsdata is free and open source, there is no account to create, and the software runs entirely on your own machine.

This policy explains how sportsdata handles personal information across the sportsdata MCP data plane, the agent workbench app, and this website.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no licence key and no payment, so there are no account, billing or entitlement records to hold. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address or payment details.
  • We do not receive your AI conversations, prompts or results. The MCP server runs on your machine and calls upstream data providers directly — nothing routes through us.
  • We do not receive your AI model key. It stays in your own client or app config.
  • We run no telemetry, no usage reporting and no crash reporting.
  • Donations happen off this site. The “Support on Ko-fi” links take you to Ko-fi, who handle any payment under their own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card details, and we receive no personal information about you from a donation beyond whatever name or message you choose to leave there.

2. Third parties you contact directly

Because the software runs locally, the network requests it makes go from your machine to the data provider. Those providers (bookmakers, prediction markets, league APIs) will see your IP address and requests, and their own privacy policies apply. We are not in the middle of that and receive no copy of it.

  • GitHub — hosts this website (GitHub Pages), the source code and the release downloads. GitHub processes standard web-server logs, including your IP address, to serve and protect those services.
  • If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we obviously receive what you send us, and we use it only to reply.
  • Authorities, where we are required by law.

We do not sell personal information, and we have nothing to sell.

3. Retention & security

We hold no user records, so there is nothing to retain, breach or delete. Correspondence you send us is kept only as long as needed to deal with it. We use reasonable technical measures to protect what little we do hold; no system is perfectly secure.

4. Children

sportsdata covers betting markets and is intended for adults 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we knowingly collect no information from anyone.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold — in practice, only correspondence you have sent us. Contact danieltomaro@icloud.com. This policy is intended to be consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

6. Cookies, tracking & this website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics by default. There are no third-party trackers, no advertising pixels, and no fingerprinting. Nothing you do on these pages is recorded by us.

If we later enable page analytics, it will be a cookie-free, privacy-preserving counter, and a consent banner will ask you first — it will not load until you accept. Our host (GitHub Pages) processes standard web-server request logs, including your IP address, to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

The MCP data plane runs on your own machine and talks directly to upstream data providers. Your prompts, your AI conversations and your model key never pass through us.
